Destination Management

Coordinating the journey from arrival to destination.

Alhussam Tourism brings decades of experience in serving pilgrims and travelers. Its services cover visa arrangements, flight tickets, hotel reservations, airport reception and ground coordination, supporting organizations that need several parts of a journey arranged together.

Hotels & Accommodation

Welcoming guests in Makkah and Madinah.

Saja Hotels is a Saudi hospitality brand built around comfortable four-star stays and a blend of traditional hospitality with accessible luxury. Its portfolio includes Saja Madinah, near the Prophet’s Mosque, and Saja Makkah in the Kudi area of Makkah.
